## Context

Neither crate is declared anywhere in the workspace. Both reach the graph through the `russh`
0.61.2 crypto stack, which is still on release-candidate RustCrypto releases:

- `chacha20 0.10.1` via `rand 0.10.1` (which `internal-russh-num-bigint 0.5.0` and
  `pageant 0.2.1` pull under `russh`, and which `oneterm-app` / `oneterm-tools` also declare
  directly) and via `ssh-cipher 0.3.0-rc.9` under `ssh-key 0.7.0-rc.10`.
- `der 0.8.0` via `ecdsa 0.17.0-rc.18`, `pkcs1 0.8.0-rc.4`, `pkcs5 0.8.0`, `pkcs8 0.11.0`,
  `sec1 0.8.1`, `spki 0.8.0`, and directly from `russh` itself.

`BUG-0056` recorded these as needing a russh-family bump. They do not: `chacha20 0.10.2` and
`der 0.8.2` were published since, and both satisfy the `^0.10` / `^0.8` requirements the whole
stack already states. The four alternatives considered and rejected — including the russh
0.61 -> 0.63.3 + russh-sftp 2.3 -> 3.0.0 manifest bump the owner asked about — are recorded in
`high-level-design.md` § "Rejected alternatives".

**No russh release is crossed, so no auth or host-key behaviour change is introduced.** The two
patch releases themselves do carry behaviour changes on adjacent paths, listed in
`high-level-design.md` § "API surface at risk": the `chacha20` SSE2-backend intrinsic fix (the
reason for the yank) and `der`'s stricter ASN.1 nesting / trailing-data handling, which sits
under key and host-key decoding. Both are upstream correctness fixes and both are covered by the
in-process loopback SSH suites in `crates/ssh/src`.
